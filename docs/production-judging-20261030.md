# Historical event judging — 30–31 October 2026

This is an anonymized engineering record of the finished competition, not a report of current server health. Actual addresses, credentials, private participant records and operator receipts are intentionally excluded.

Round 1 publication completed fifty questions. Round 2 was revised to an easier 100-question pack after Round 1. Its recorded publication finished 3,600 real expected validation checks and 400 clues, preserving the old harder drafts and Round 1 sporting records. Temporary publication access was disabled after finalization and backup verification.

The judge was qualified with legacy and batch kernel probes, then adopted by the application coordinator under the normal live lease and clock guards. Compile-once participant batches and sixteen physical slots were enabled for the event release. The final fifteen-team benchmark achieved a 15.683-second official median and 22.254-second peak; under ten seconds was not achieved.

Recovery preserved original source/verdict bindings, failed attempts, unknown outcomes, positive physical cleanup evidence and consumed permits. Transport failures were reconciled through the original receipt; publication did not invent a new validation to replace an already passed run. Some source compiler errors had been misclassified as infrastructure faults; reviewed diagnostic attribution was corrected and covered by regression fixtures.
