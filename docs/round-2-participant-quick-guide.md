Report by **9:30 AM** with your **laptop and charger**. Late arrivals get less time.

## Play

- Open https://8-231-75-42.sslip.io/ and use your team code. Choose your own member.
- Languages: **C, C++, Java or Python**. Attempt the 100 bugs in any order.
- Fix the given buggy code. **Run samples** is practice; **Submit fix** is official.
- Normally, the **first correct server-received submission** wins the bug. Opening it does not reserve it. Each team can earn a bug only once.
- Teammates share coins, points, hints and items. Solo entrants work alone.

## Coins, hints and points

Unspent Round 1 coins carry over; Round 2 points start at zero. An eligible solve
adds the full **250-1,000 coin bounty** to your wallet. Purchases spend coins.

**Hints are bought in order.** Additional costs: **10%, 30%, 80%** of bounty.
All three cost **120% total**. Highest assistance gives **100% / 90% / 70% / 50%**
of base points for no hint / Hint 1 / Hint 2 / Hint 3. Probe counts as Hint 1;
Scanner as Hint 2. Use the highest tier; reductions do not stack.

An incorrect official submission adds **5 minutes to ranking time**, not a lockout.
Samples and infrastructure errors have no such penalty. Ranking: **points**, then
**cumulative solve time + penalties**, then **harder solved bugs**.
