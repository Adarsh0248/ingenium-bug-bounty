# Verdant Nexus

The GRAPHS area is a directly drawn living data network. Glass processing cells
connect to a Root Core through branching curved links. Mint, violet and cyan
packets flow across the map; the labels describe graph traversal and buffers.
The region keeps its existing `verdant` identity, GRAPHS category, four-by-four
map size and pan-only controls. Its participant and staff display name is
**Verdant Nexus**.

These effects are cosmetic local footprints; they do not modify graph state,
other targets, question difficulty, rewards or judging. Each anomaly retains one
clickable core. Decorative components and signal effects ignore pointer hits.
Existing rarity thresholds control size and the new Branching signal aura;
family proportions also distinguish the small Bitrunner from the long Leviathan.

## Performance and interaction

- At most 49 network nodes and 12 animated packet paths, including large maps.
- Body, moving parts and reaction textures are cached per family: 18 shared GPU
  textures. Moving components use one instanced draw per creature, with no new lights.
- Movement uses the shared independent full-region routes and direction changes.
- Pause, hover, an open workbench and reduced motion freeze the applicable animation.
- The synthetic preview retains ten bugs per area with all six families and all
  bounty tiers. Its questions and judging remain dummy data.

Unit checks cover connected acyclic layouts, bounded geometry, family identity,
rarity and articulation. Browser checks inspect the actual rendered parts, cached
art, packet animation, pause/hover, mobile layout and question entry, alongside
the other regions' interaction regressions. Physical old-laptop frame rates
remain unmeasured.
